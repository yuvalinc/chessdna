# Chess DNA — AI Chess Analysis

**[chessdna.app](https://chessdna.app)** turns every chess game into progress.

Chess DNA imports your games from **Chess.com** and **Lichess** and uses AI + Stockfish to:

- 🧬 **Find your patterns** — the recurring mistakes that quietly cost you games
- 🎯 **Fix your weaknesses** — a personalized skill profile across 8 dimensions (tactics, endgames, time management…)
- ♟️ **Replay your mistakes** — step back through the moments that mattered, with engine analysis and plain-English explanations

👉 **Try it free at [chessdna.app](https://chessdna.app)**
